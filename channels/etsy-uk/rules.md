# Etsy UK channel rules (plain-English companion to governance/policies/etsy-uk-compliance.yml)
Checked 2026-11-12 against Etsy's Creativity Standards, the drop-shipping and reselling help page and the production partner help page. Not legal advice; the owner confirms the policy file.

## What may be listed
- Curated gift boxes: a themed assortment of different item types from more than one brand, personally selected and creatively packaged into a new set. Every item is listed in the description. Single-brand boxes, single-item-type boxes and mystery boxes do not qualify.
- Our original designs made by a disclosed production partner: bodysuits, muslins, prints, milestone cards. Seller-prompted AI designs are allowed and must be disclosed in the description.
- Buyer-personalised items made by a production partner: name bodysuits, engraved wooden items. The first photo shows a finished example.

## What may not be listed
- Any wholesale item sold as it is (the ebay_resale lane). Reselling and drop shipping are not allowed, except craft and party supplies, which we do not sell.
- Medical drugs, regulated medical devices, and anything claiming to treat, prevent or cure a condition. Disclaimers do not help. Stage 2 products never come here.
- Recalled items and items that pose a choking, strangulation or electrocution hazard.

## Listing hygiene
- Disclose production partners on the listing and in shop settings. State where the item ships from.
- Photos are ours. No supplier photos for gift boxes or designed items.
- Processing time matches reality: in-stock boxes 1 to 2 days, made-to-order items up to 7 days.
- Fees apply to postage as well as the item. Opt out of Offsite Ads while under the threshold.

## API
- Seller App only, for our own shop. Default limits 10,000 requests a day and 10 a second. Respect Etsy's caching and branding rules. Public listing searches are market context for the owner, not a pricing engine.
