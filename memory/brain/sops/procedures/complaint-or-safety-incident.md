# Procedure: Complaint or safety incident
A cross-department procedure. It never overrides company rules, governance/ or the approval tiers. A person approves anything live.

## Purpose
Act fast when a customer reports harm, a dangerous product or a serious complaint.

## Trigger
A message mentions injury, fire, allergy, a legal threat, a chargeback or similar. It arrives as a critical alert.

## Roles
- the owner leads
- customer care
- compliance
- sales

## Steps
1. Read the alert today. Do not reply from a template.
2. If anyone may be at risk, pause the listing straight away and stop shipping that product.
3. Reply personally and kindly. Ask for the order ID and a photo, never an address or card details. Do not admit liability or give medical or legal advice.
4. Record exactly what was reported and what you did, with times.
5. Check the product against safety marking and supplier documents. Ask the supplier for them.
6. Look at what the law and the platform require for unsafe products, and whether the incident must be reported. Take advice from Trading Standards or a solicitor if unsure.
7. Decide on a refund, a recall of similar orders and a fix. Record the outcome and update the compliance rules if something was missed.

## Records
- Incident notes with dates
- Supplier documents
- The decision

## Escalate to the owner
- Everything: the owner leads

## Done when
- [ ] Risk removed
- [ ] Customer replied to by a person
- [ ] Cause and fix recorded
