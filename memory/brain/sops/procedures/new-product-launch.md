# Procedure: New product launch
A cross-department procedure. It never overrides company rules, governance/ or the approval tiers. A person approves anything live.

## Purpose
Take a product from idea to a live listing safely and profitably.

## Trigger
The research department has a product that passed the rules and the owner wants to launch it.

## Roles
- research and sourcing
- compliance and reviewer
- listings and pricing
- marketing
- the owner approves

## Steps
1. Import the market data and supplier quote with research.py import. The rules reject banned items, slow delivery and margins under 30 percent, and keep the reason.
2. Run the four reviews (listings, pricing, compliance, finance) and attach each reply. A price suggestion that breaks the margin blocks the product.
3. Tick your own checks on the Approvals page, read every flag, then approve or deny with a reason. Approve makes a launch pack. It buys and lists nothing.
4. Order one or two samples. Follow the sample-order checklist: real delivery time, quality, your own photos, safety marking.
5. Mark stock_in_hand, re-run the margin with real costs, and finalise the listing and price. Compliance checks it.
6. List it by hand. Record it with watch.py listing add so supplier price changes are caught.
7. Marketing plans the first posts with the social-post-approval procedure.

## Records
- The dossier and launch pack in research/
- The decision and reason
- The listing recorded in watch.py

## Escalate to the owner
- Any block from the gate
- A sample that fails on quality or safety

## Done when
- [ ] Sample checked
- [ ] Listing live and recorded
- [ ] First posts planned
