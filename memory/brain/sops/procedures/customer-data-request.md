# Procedure: Customer data request
A cross-department procedure. It never overrides company rules, governance/ or the approval tiers. A person approves anything live.

## Purpose
Answer a request to see, correct or delete someone's personal data inside the legal time limit.

## Trigger
A customer asks what data you hold, asks you to correct it, or asks you to delete it.

## Roles
- the owner
- compliance

## Steps
1. Log the request the day it arrives: who (by customer reference), what they asked for, and the date. You normally have one month to respond.
2. Check it really is them. Reply to the contact details you already hold, and ask for nothing more than you need.
3. For access: collect everything held about them (customer record, orders, messages, consents) and send it in a clear format. For deletion: check what you must keep (for example tax records), then remove or anonymise the rest.
4. For correction: fix it and tell them.
5. Reply in writing before the deadline and record what you did. If you cannot meet the deadline, say why and when.
6. If the request is unclear or you refuse part of it, take advice. Never ignore it.

## Records
- The request log, the date sent and what was sent or deleted

## Escalate to the owner
- Every request goes to the owner at once

## Done when
- [ ] Request answered inside the deadline
- [ ] Record kept
