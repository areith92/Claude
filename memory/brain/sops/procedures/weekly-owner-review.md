# Procedure: Weekly owner review
A cross-department procedure. It never overrides company rules, governance/ or the approval tiers. A person approves anything live.

## Purpose
A calm 30 minutes each week to see how the business is really doing.

## Trigger
Once a week, at the same time.

## Roles
- the owner
- the orchestrator

## Steps
1. Open Operations. Read every open alert and close or acknowledge it with what you did.
2. Check Deliveries: any parcel late, any supplier repeatedly slow.
3. Read profit and loss for the week: earned, spent, profit, and which orders lost money.
4. Check Social engagement and the shop funnel: what worked, and the abandoned checkouts.
5. Clear Approvals and the customer drafts. Fill in any missing facts for the reply templates.
6. Run orchestrate.py digest, then set next week's goal with orchestrate.py plan.
7. Write one lesson and one decision into memory/brain/.

## Records
- The digest and your decisions

## Escalate to the owner
- Anything you cannot explain

## Done when
- [ ] Alerts cleared
- [ ] Next week planned
