# Procedure: Social post approval
A cross-department procedure. It never overrides company rules, governance/ or the approval tiers. A person approves anything live.

## Purpose
Make sure every post is honest, legal and approved before it goes out.

## Trigger
Marketing has drafted a post or an ad campaign.

## Roles
- marketing
- compliance
- the owner approves

## Steps
1. Marketing writes a brief: product, hook, caption, hashtags, call to action, shot list and the media to use.
2. Media must be your own photos or video of the real product. AI images may only be backgrounds or graphics, and must be labelled where the platform requires it.
3. Compliance checks it against the social selling rules: true price and delivery claims, no invented reviews or scarcity, paid or gifted promotion labelled (for example #ad), and product safety where relevant.
4. You approve or send it back with a reason.
5. Publish by hand in the platform, or schedule it in Meta Business Suite. Never from the agents.
6. Import the platform's figures later (watch.py metrics import) so the dashboard shows how it did.
7. For an ad campaign: check the monthly total stays inside 100 GBP, set the limit inside the platform too, and record the spend.

## Records
- The approved brief and the metrics

## Escalate to the owner
- A claim you cannot back up
- Any campaign over the cap

## Done when
- [ ] Approved and published by hand
- [ ] Figures imported
