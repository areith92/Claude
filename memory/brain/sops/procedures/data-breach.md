# Procedure: Data breach
A cross-department procedure. It never overrides company rules, governance/ or the approval tiers. A person approves anything live.

## Purpose
Contain and report a loss or exposure of personal data quickly.

## Trigger
Any sign that personal data was lost, sent to the wrong person, exposed, or accessed by someone who should not have it (including a leaked key).

## Roles
- the owner leads
- tech support
- compliance

## Steps
1. Write down what you know and when you found out. The clock for reporting starts then.
2. Contain it: revoke the key or password, remove the exposed file or message, and stop any process involved.
3. Work out what data, how many people, and how likely harm is.
4. If the breach is likely to put people at risk, report it to the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware. If the risk is high, tell the people affected without delay. The ICO has a short checker for this.
5. Record every breach, even if you do not report it: what happened, the effect and what you did.
6. Fix the cause, change the key, and add a test or a rule so it cannot happen the same way again.

## Records
- The breach log in docs/privacy/ and any ICO reference

## Escalate to the owner
- Everything: the owner leads

## Done when
- [ ] Contained
- [ ] Reported if required
- [ ] Cause fixed and recorded
